Privacy Policy & GDPR

We are a collective that uses art and evidence to shed light on the tobacco industry's manipulative tactics. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. The Data We Collect

When you visit our website and participate in our survey, we may collect, use, store, and transfer different kinds of personal data about you:

  • Identity Data: First name, last name, social media handle.
  • Contact Data: Email address.
  • Technical Data: IP address and browser type.
  • Special Category Data: Your survey responses, including your personal stories, health-related information regarding tobacco/nicotine use, and any photos you upload.

2. How We Use Your Data

We will only use your personal data when the law allows us to. We use your data to:

  • Process your survey responses and generate your confirmation email.
  • Store your uploaded images securely.
  • Use your anonymized or submitted stories for the BFWE art project.
  • Analyze website traffic via cookies to improve our site.

3. Cookies and Consent

We use cookies to ensure basic site functionality and to analyze how visitors interact with our website. You have the choice to accept or refuse these analytics cookies via our cookie banner. If you refuse, we will not track your behavior for analytics purposes. However, if you choose to submit the survey, your survey data will still be recorded to fulfill the purpose of the art project.

4. Data Security & Storage

We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way. We store your data in a secure, access-controlled environment.

5. Your Legal Rights

Under data protection laws, you have rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the data we hold on you.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request that we delete all your personal data, including your stories and uploaded pictures, from our database and servers.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw your consent to our use of cookies at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at bxl.proj@gmail.com. We will respond to all legitimate requests within one month.
